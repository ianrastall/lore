# VedAstro list: 77 figures added, 31 October 2026

77 people from the [master list](celebrity-master-list-2026-10-31.md), with their birth data taken
from [VedAstro's list of famous people](https://huggingface.co/datasets/vedastro-org/15000-Famous-People-Birth-Date-Location).
The library goes from 1,330 figures to 1,407. Each one is credited to Astro-Databank and VedAstro,
and keeps the AA rating the list gives. Wikidata supplied the item, the sitelinks and the dates of
death. The biographies are new.

Every birthplace was compared with Wikidata. The list's times were kept as they stand.
