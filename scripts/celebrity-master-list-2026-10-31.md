## For whoever researches these

- Gemini chose by fame, not by birth data. The library rule still applies: Rodden AA or A only.
  Earlier batches found most current English-language names have no reliable time, so expect a
  low yield; a name with no qualifying time stays out.
- Every entry needs `deathDate` as well (see Contemporaries in `docs/next-steps.md`).
- Morrissey, Sam Smith and Tim Cook were not really checked on 7 October: Astro-Databank returned
  a namesake. They are on the list.
- Steve McQueen and John Adams are marked (director, composer). The library's Steve McQueen is
  the actor, and the rejected John Adams was the president.
- Brian Cox appears twice, marked actor and physicist.
